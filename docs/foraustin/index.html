Three days. One idea.

Friday is about funding it. Saturday is about living it. Sunday is about understanding it. Come to one of the three, or come to all of them.

Everything raised across the weekend goes to the next generation — kids, students, and the work Northpoint does alongside them in Williamson County and Greater Austin. The number Northpoint settles on replaces this range, and a live total can sit beside it through the weekend.

The church behind it

FOR Austin is Northpoint.

Fifteen years ago, 24 adults and 32 kids set out to start a church that was For Austin. For too long the church has been known for what it is against — we would rather be known for what we are for.

If any of this made you curious, the simplest next step is a Sunday. Come as you are, grab a coffee on us, and leave in about an hour.
