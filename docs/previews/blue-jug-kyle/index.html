02 — What’s in the filter

Nine stages,
one pour.

Most water stores will tell you their water is “filtered.” Here is the actual mineral bed every gallon passes through, in order.

01
Calcium SulfiteCaSO₃
Strips the chlorine out first, so nothing downstream has to fight it.
02
TourmalinePiezoelectric
A tridymite–tourmaline stage that puts a natural charge into the water.
03
ZirconIon
Ionic exchange — the energy stage of the bed.
04
Magnesium OxideMgO + catalyst
Raises hydrogen and pH while dropping ORP. This is the stage that makes the water alkaline.
05
QuartzTaste
The far-infrared stage. This is the one you notice on the tongue.
06
CalciteCa · Mg
Puts calcium and magnesium back in — minerals reverse osmosis takes out.
07
HematiteAnion
Negative-ion energy stage.
08
ZeoliteExchange
A natural mineral polish before the final stage.
09
SilverAntibacterial
The last word — keeps the bed and the pour clean.
